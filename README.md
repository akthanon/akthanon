## 🧠 How I Approach Problems

I tend to learn and investigate through a simple loop:

**observe → formulate hypotheses → experiment → break → analyze → automate → repeat**

I am particularly drawn to problems where the obvious interface hides a more interesting underlying mechanism.

Rather than treating tools as black boxes, I prefer understanding **what they are actually doing, why they work, and where their assumptions stop being valid**.

This applies to cybersecurity, programming, reverse engineering, systems, and pretty much anything else that catches my curiosity.
